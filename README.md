### Connecting Your New Custom Domain (When Purchased):
Whenever you purchase your domain name (such as .pk or any other registrar):
1. In your Netlify site dashboard, click 'Domain management' -> 'Add custom domain'.
2. Type your purchased domain name.
3. Follow the 1-step DNS instructions (e.g. set nameservers or CNAME).
Netlify automatically issues free SSL certificates and handles everything at zero hosting cost.
