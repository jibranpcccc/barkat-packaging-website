Kharboza carton and kharboza box specification

A melon is heavy, round and easy to bruise. Whether you call it a kharboza box, a melon box, a melon carton or a melon crate, the carton has to carry the weight, breathe, and lock together in a stack.

Load
12–20 kg

Built for field harvest loads and long-distance road transport.

Board
5-Ply BC

Heavy 5-ply BC-flute with a high edge crush test rating.

Airflow
Vented

Convective side vents stop heat building up and rotting the fruit.

Handling
Hand-holes

Reinforced die-cut hand-holes for rapid loading at the farm.

Why corrugated instead of a wooden crate

Wooden crates splinter, and every splinter is a bruise on a melon. Corrugated kharboza cartons remove that risk, weigh less on the truck, and stack with interlocking tabs so the columns stay locked in transit.

  • No wood splinters and no bruising
  • Lighter than wood, so more payload per truck
  • Interlocking tabs lock columns on rough roads
  • Vented sides keep the stack cool

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a kharboza carton?

A kharboza carton is a heavy-duty ventilated corrugated crate used to move melons from the field to market without crushing the bottom layers.

What weight does a kharboza carton hold?

Our kharboza and melon crates are built for 12kg to 20kg loads in heavy 5-ply BC-flute board with a high edge crush test rating.

Why use corrugated crates instead of wooden crates for melons?

Corrugated crates eliminate wood splinters and the bruising they cause, weigh less, and stack with interlocking tabs so columns stay locked in transit.

Are the melon crates ventilated?

Yes. Convective side vents keep airflow moving through the stack so heat does not build up and rot the fruit.

What is the minimum order for kharboza cartons?

The minimum wholesale order is 500 pieces, ex-factory Hyderabad.

Where do you deliver kharboza cartons?

We supply farms and mandis around Thatta, Badin, Matiari and Hala, and deliver across Sindh from our S.I.T.E. Hyderabad factory.
