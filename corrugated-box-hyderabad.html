What corrugated box sizes do you make?

Standard sizes run from 8x6x4 inches for courier parcels up to 24x18x18 inches for 7-ply export containers, all measured internally. Custom sizes are made to order.

What is the difference between 3-ply, 5-ply and 7-ply?

3-ply is single wall for loads up to roughly 14 kg, 5-ply double wall covers 25 to 45 kg, and 7-ply triple wall carries 80 to 120 kg.

What is the minimum order quantity?
