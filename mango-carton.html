Built for cold chain, not just for shipping

A mango carton has to survive humidity, stacking and 21 days at sea. We build with moisture-resistant kraft liners, precision ventilation and board grades matched to the load, so the bottom layer arrives as firm as the top.

  • Ventilation: convective side slots on the 4kg GCC carton
  • Stacking: telescopic two-piece 5kg carton tested through 21-day reefer transit
  • Board: 3-ply B-flute up to 5-ply heavy BC-flute
  • Printing: one to four colour flexographic brand printing

Frequently Asked Questions

What sizes of mango carton do you manufacture?

We manufacture 2kg, 4kg, 5kg and 10kg mango cartons. Internal sizes are 12x8x4 inches, 14.5x9.5x4.5 inches, 15x10x5 inches and 18x12x7 inches respectively.

Are your mango cartons ventilated for export?

Yes. The 4kg GCC carton carries six convective side-ventilation slots, and the 5kg carton uses a telescopic two-piece design tested for multi-tier pallet stacking through 21 days of cold-chain ocean transit.

What is the minimum order quantity for mango cartons?

The minimum wholesale order is 500 pieces, ex-factory Hyderabad.

Can you print our brand on the mango carton?

Yes. We print in one to four colours by flexographic press, including white coated top liner for high-gloss brand printing on the 2kg presentation box.

Where do you deliver mango cartons in Sindh?

We supply orchards and packhouses across Mirpur Khas, Tando Allahyar, Matiari, Hyderabad and the Karachi port area, plus road freight to Punjab and cross-border to Central Asia.

What do mango cartons cost?

The 5kg mango export carton is published from PKR 85 to 115 per piece. Final price depends on board grade, printing colours and order quantity.
