Hydraulic burst tester clamps board samples and measures the hydrostatic pressure (in kg/cm² and PSI) required to burst the paper web.

Evaluates the vertical compression strength of the fluted column to verify stacking safety factors on multi-tier warehouse pallets.

Measures liquid penetration into liners to guarantee cold-chain survival inside humid sea containers (Cobb < 28 g/m²).
