About Barkat Packaging

Over 24 years of dependable corrugated box manufacturing, converting, and bulk carton supply for industries across Sindh.

Direct Corrugation Mill in Hyderabad

Established in May 2000 at Plot F 9, S.I.T.E. Industrial Area, Hyderabad, Barkat Packaging has operated for over two decades as a trusted manufacturing partner for Sindh's industrial sector. We manufacture 3-ply single wall, 5-ply double wall, and 7-ply heavy duty corrugated cartons from raw kraft paper reels.

Our factory is strategically located with quick access to the Indus Highway and Hyderabad Bypass, allowing smooth daily dispatches to textile mills in Kotri, FMCG and industrial manufacturers in Hyderabad, pharmaceutical units, and the major agricultural mango harvest corridors of Mirpur Khas, Tando Allahyar, and Sanghar.

Unlike brokers or middlemen who outsource production, Barkat Packaging operates its own converting machinery on-site — including high-speed corrugation, heavy industrial platen die-cutting, precision rotary slotting, automated wire stitching, and gluing units.

Quality Inspection & Board Testing

We verify that every production batch meets agreed paper grammage, burst strength, and edge crush specifications.

Bursting Strength (Mullen BST)

Hydraulic burst testing clamps board samples to measure the pressure (in kg/cm² and PSI) required to burst the carton wall, ensuring the box will not puncture during transit.

Edge Crush Test (ECT)

Measures the vertical compression strength of the fluted column to ensure cartons can be safely stacked 5 to 7 tiers high on warehouse pallets without corner buckling.

Cobb Moisture Test

Measures water absorption of kraft liner paper to guarantee that cold-chain mango boxes maintain structural stiffness inside 90% humidity refrigerated sea containers.
