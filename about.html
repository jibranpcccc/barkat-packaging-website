Sindh's Trusted Corrugated Box Factory

For nearly a quarter-century, Barkat Packaging has provided Sindh’s textile mills, agro-exporters, and industrial manufacturers with direct-from-mill corrugated cartons — cutting out middleman commissions and eliminating the 160-km freight penalty of sourcing from Karachi.

Factory Infrastructure & Plant Machinery

In-house converting lines for high-speed manufacturing.

Continuous Board Production

High-Speed Corrugation Line

Multi-ply continuous corrugator bonding virgin Kraft paper with steam heat and organic starch glue. Converts B, C, E, BC double-wall, and 7-ply heavy boards.

CAD Die-Cutting & Creasing

Precision Platen Die-Cutting Press

Heavy industrial platen die-cutter achieving ±0.5mm accuracy for self-locking mailers, ventilated mango export boxes, and custom internal partition grids.

Tooling & Printing

Custom Die Tooling & Flexo Presses

In-house wooden die blocks and high-definition flexographic printing for company branding, legal regulatory icons, and batch barcodes.

Structural Fastening

Heavy-Duty Box Wire Stitching

Galvanized high-tensile steel wire stitching and automated folder-gluers providing maximum joint shear strength for 5-ply master export shipping cartons.

In-House Quality Control Laboratory

Every production lot is lab-calibrated before dispatch.

Bursting Strength (BST)

Digital Mullen tester applying hydraulic pressure to verify puncture resistance in kg/cm² and PSI.

Edge Crush Test (ECT)

Measures fluting column crush strength to certify multi-tier warehouse pallet stacking safety.

Cobb Water Absorptiveness

Cobb 60-second sizing tester ensuring cold-chain boxes resist 90% humidity during reefer transit.
