Cold-Chain Mango & Fruit Export Boxes

Moisture-resistant corrugated cartons engineered for Sindhri mangoes and fresh fruit exporters across Mirpur Khas, Tando Allahyar, Hyderabad, and Sanghar.

Engineered for 90% Humidity Reefer Shipping

During sea container export to the UK, EU, UAE, and Saudi Arabia, fresh mangoes are transported in refrigerated containers (reefers) under continuous humidity. Standard corrugated boxes absorb moisture from the chilled air, causing their fluting to soften and cartons to collapse under pallet weight.

Barkat Packaging manufactures agricultural cartons using moisture-resistant kraft paper to prevent boxes from softening inside humid cold storage and refrigerated sea containers.

Ventilation holes allow cold air to move around the fruit during refrigerated transport, keeping produce fresh and preventing condensation.
