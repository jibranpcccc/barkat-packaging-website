Engineered for 90% Humidity Reefer Shipping & Heavy Orchard Stacking

During sea container export to the UK, EU, UAE, and Saudi Arabia, fresh mangoes, dates, and produce are transported in refrigerated containers (reefers) under continuous humidity. Standard corrugated boxes absorb moisture from chilled air, causing their fluting to soften and cartons to collapse under pallet weight.

Barkat Packaging manufactures agricultural cartons using hydrophobic moisture-resistant kraft paper to prevent boxes from softening inside humid cold storage, refrigerated sea containers, or open farm transport trucks across Sindh.

Precision die-cut ventilation slots allow refrigerated airflow to circulate around fruits, maintaining freshness, preventing sweating, and ensuring produce arrives in prime export condition.
