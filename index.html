Frequently Asked Questions About Our Carton Factory

Answers to common questions regarding corrugated box manufacturing, wholesale ordering, factory location, and dispatch across Sindh.

Q: Which is the leading corrugated box manufacturer and carton factory in Hyderabad?

Barkat Packaging is the leading corrugated box manufacturer and carton factory in S.I.T.E. Industrial Area, Hyderabad, Sindh (Plot F 9, Postal Code 71800). Established in 2000 with a daily production capacity exceeding 50,000 cartons, we manufacture 3-ply, 5-ply, and 7-ply shipping boxes, export mango cartons, and custom printed packaging at direct factory wholesale rates.

Q: Where is Barkat Packaging carton factory located in Hyderabad?

Our factory is located at Plot F 9, S.I.T.E. Industrial Area, Hyderabad, Sindh (71800). Our facility is easily accessible via Indus Highway and Hyderabad Bypass with dedicated loading bays for container trucks and commercial freight vehicles.

Q: What types of corrugated cartons does your Hyderabad factory manufacture?

We manufacture 3-ply single wall cartons (for e-commerce and retail goods), 5-ply double wall master shipping cartons (for industrial logistics, textiles, and FMCG), 7-ply triple wall containers (for heavy machinery), ventilated cold-chain mango export boxes, and custom die-cut inner partitions with precision flexographic printing.

Q: Can businesses order custom-sized cartons directly from the factory?

Yes. Barkat Packaging provides factory-direct custom box manufacturing. Businesses can specify exact internal dimensions (Length × Width × Height), flute types (B-Flute, C-Flute, E-Flute), bursting factor (BF), and paper grammage (120 to 250 GSM). Minimum order quantity (MOQ) starts from 500 boxes.

Q: What delivery coverage is available from your Hyderabad carton factory?

We provide fast 24 to 48 hour scheduled truck delivery across S.I.T.E. Hyderabad, Kotri Industrial Area, Jamshoro, Latifabad, Qasimabad, Mirpur Khas, Tando Allahyar, Nooriabad, and throughout the Sindh agricultural and industrial corridor.

Q: Where can I find a gatta factory near me in Hyderabad for wholesale empty cartons? (گٹہ فیکٹری حیدرآباد)

Barkat Packaging is the premier gatta factory located at Plot F 9, S.I.T.E. Industrial Area, Hyderabad. Whether you are a local shopkeeper, online seller, or manufacturing plant seeking khali carton boxes (empty corrugated cartons), you can purchase directly from our factory floor at wholesale rates without middleman commissions. Truck deliveries and on-site pickups are available Monday through Saturday.

Q: Does Barkat Packaging manufacture Khajoor (Dates) and Kharboza (Melon) packaging boxes in Sindh?

Yes. Beyond mango packaging, we supply food-grade moisture-barrier corrugated boxes for dates and khajoor (for growers and processors in Khairpur Mirs, Sukkur, and Rohri) ranging from 1kg retail packs to 5kg and 10kg master cartons. We also produce heavy-gauge 5-ply ventilated crates for Kharboza (muskmelons) and farm vegetables for growers across Thatta, Badin, Matiari, and Hala.

Q: What is the price of empty carton boxes in Hyderabad and how is it calculated?

Corrugated carton box prices are calculated based on surface sheet area, ply construction (3-ply, 5-ply, or 7-ply), paper grammage (GSM 120–250), and order volume. Standard 3-ply small cartons generally range from PKR 25 to PKR 45, while heavy-duty 5-ply master cartons range between PKR 65 and PKR 150 depending on specifications. You can use our on-page Box Size & Price Calculator for an immediate estimate or WhatsApp us at 0333 2622963 for a customized commercial quotation.

Q: Does Barkat Packaging manufacture flat cardboard sheets and gatta layer pads in Hyderabad?

Yes. Barkat Packaging manufactures precision-cut flat corrugated sheets and gatta layer pads at Plot F 9, S.I.T.E. Industrial Area, Hyderabad. Available in single-wall (3-ply) and double-wall (5-ply) in popular sizes like 24×36", 30×40", 36×48", and 40×48", or cut to your exact custom specifications. Ideal for pallet separator pads, furniture surface protection, art framing, and crate dividers at direct factory wholesale rates.

Q: Where can I buy empty boxes for house shifting, garment packing, and moving near me in Hyderabad?

You can purchase empty carton boxes for packing, house shifting, office moving, and garment/dress packaging directly from Barkat Packaging in S.I.T.E. Industrial Area, Hyderabad. We supply durable small (14×10×10"), medium (18×14×12"), and large wardrobe (24×18×18") corrugated boxes with reinforced corners and optional die-cut carry hand-holes. Available flat-packed in bundles of 25 for quick on-site pickup or city delivery across Hyderabad.
